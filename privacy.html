Privacy Policy.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how Nexovent Tech (“we”, “us”) handles information when you visit our website or contact contact@nexovent.tech.

Information you provide

The project planner and contact form may ask for your name, email, business name, project needs, budget, timeline, links and message. The current static forms prepare a draft in your own email application; they do not transmit form entries to a Nexovent server. Information reaches us only if you choose to send the email.

How information is used

We use enquiries to respond, understand project requirements, prepare estimates and communicate about services. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and third parties

This site may load third-party assets such as hosted fonts. Those providers may receive technical information, including your IP address, when your browser requests assets. Hosting providers may keep standard security logs. We do not claim to use analytics or advertising cookies unless specifically configured.

Retention and security

We retain enquiries for a reasonable period for business and legal purposes. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not send passwords or payment card details by ordinary email.

Your choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information. Contact us to make a request.

External links and changes

Third-party sites have their own privacy practices. We may update this policy by publishing a revised date on this page.
